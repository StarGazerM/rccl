# Code of Conduct

We aim for a respectful, technically rigorous project. Participants must
avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted contact.
Critique code, evidence, and design; do not target people.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to
keep discussions safe and productive. Report concerns privately through the
security/reporting channel in `SECURITY.md` rather than escalating a public
thread.

This code applies to project spaces and project-related interactions. By
participating, you agree to cooperate with maintainers and follow the
repository's Apache-2.0 OR MIT licensing terms.
